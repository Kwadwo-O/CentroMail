A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you settle in.

A little clarity goes a long way.

What can I connect?add

Connect Gmail for email, and set up Telegram, WhatsApp Business, or a Discord bot from the integrations page. Some connections need a developer account or access token; the setup guides explain what you need.

Can I make it my own?add

Absolutely. Settings lets you choose your theme, accent colour, font, text size, corner style, and motion preferences. You can also reduce animations for a quieter experience.

How does CentroMail handle my data?add

CentroMail stores account details and cached messages in its application database, and talks to providers to load and send messages. You can disconnect integrations or delete your account in settings. Read our privacy policy for the details.
