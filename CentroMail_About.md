# CentroMail

**Your conversations. One place.**

CentroMail is a unified communications application designed to bring email and supported messaging services together in one organised place.

Instead of switching between different applications to keep up with conversations, CentroMail aims to provide a single space where people can manage their communications, connect multiple accounts and reply from the appropriate service.

## What CentroMail aims to offer

- **A unified inbox** — bring supported conversations from different services into one interface.
- **Multiple accounts** — manage more than one email account in the same application.
- **Connected conversations** — read and reply to messages using the relevant connected account or service.
- **Service integrations** — connect providers through supported APIs and protocols.
- **Local messaging** — explore private messaging between CentroMail users, including self-hosted or local-network use.
- **A customisable experience** — use a clean, responsive interface inspired by Material 3 Expressive.
- **Privacy-conscious design** — keep account connections and permissions under the user's control.

## The vision

CentroMail's goal is to make digital communication feel less fragmented. Email and supported messaging services should be accessible from one consistent experience, without losing track of which account or service a conversation belongs to.

The project also aims to leave room for customisation, community-developed integrations and optional smart organisation features.

## Integrations

CentroMail is being designed to support different services through integrations. Potential examples include Gmail, Outlook and other email providers, alongside messaging platforms where suitable official APIs or supported protocols are available.

Integration capabilities depend on each provider. Some services restrict third-party access or do not allow external applications to read or send messages, so support will vary.

## Design

CentroMail takes inspiration from Google's Material 3 Expressive design language. Its intended experience is modern, responsive and easy to navigate, with expressive shapes, thoughtful interactions and customisation options.

## Project status

CentroMail is currently in development. Its features, integrations and design may evolve as the project progresses. Planned capabilities should not be assumed to be available until they have been implemented and documented.
